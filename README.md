### 🧭 About me

- 🖥️ I build **native, multi-platform applications** with Delphi and the **FMX framework**, shipping to **Windows, Linux, Android and iOS** from a single codebase.
- 🤖 Exploring **AI-assisted development**: my Rust projects are built with AI coding tools while I focus on design, testing and direction.

- 📦 I publish small, focused **Delphi libraries** for things I couldn't find done well elsewhere.

### 🏭 Professional work — Teraoka SA

I develop and maintain **production software for [Teraoka (DIGI)](https://www.digisystem.com/) South Africa**, running daily at food manufacturers, meat and poultry processors, and major retail chains across Southern Africa.

- ⚖️ **Industrial weighing & labelling:** applications for DIGI DI-760/770/771 indicator terminals covering live receiving, grading, check-weighing, despatch and sequential label printing on factory floors.
- 🔌 **Hardware integration:** Delphi APIs and protocol layers for DIGI scales over TCP and serial, RF stations, and REST services that expose live scale weights to other systems.
- 🏷️ **Retail & ESL:** import services for electronic shelf labels, retail scale data sync (DigiConnect) for Windows and Linux, and Android ESL and label-printing apps.
- 🛠️ **Business systems:** service-call management (desktop, Android and web), licence servers and portals, reporting services, live production totals dashboards, and stock and fuel management.
- 🔄 **Modernisation:** migrating long-running Delphi 7 / XE4 systems to modern Delphi (10–13) and adding web and mobile front-ends.

<sub>Client work lives in private repositories, so it doesn't appear in the public project list below.</sub>
